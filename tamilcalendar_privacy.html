Privacy Policy

For Android apps published by Dipali Nandecha

Effective from 1 October 2026 | Last updated 1 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how information is handled when using our Tamil Calendar mobile application.

Summary: No account is required, no personal identity details are sent to private servers, and third-party data processing is restricted to Google AdMob for ad delivery and app reliability diagnostics.

Apps Covered by this Policy

  • Tamil Calendar & Panchangam (com.tamilcalendarpanchangam)

Information the App Collects

Information You Provide

None. The app functions without registration or login. Your preferences (selected location, custom event reminders, and theme preferences) remain stored locally on your device storage.

Location Data

Solar and lunar calculations (such as Nalla Neram, Raghu Kaalam, Kuligai, and daily Thithi) rely on geographic coordinates. You can select your city manually from the available list. If GPS access is granted, the app reads your coordinates locally to identify the nearest city. Your precise coordinates are never recorded or transmitted to our servers.

Advertising & Diagnostics

Google AdMob services are incorporated to show ads. Google may process:

  • Advertising IDs and relevant hardware parameters;
  • IP address for coarse geographical matching;
  • Interactions with ad creatives;
  • Diagnostic crash reports to improve application stability.

Consult Google's policies directly via how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Device Permissions Requested

  • Internet Access: Necessary to display advertisements and synchronize calendar updates.
  • Location (Optional): Used strictly to identify local sunrise, sunset, and Nalla Neram timings.
  • Notifications (Optional): Used to trigger reminders for Tamil festivals, Viratham days, and Amavasai/Pournami.

Children's Privacy

This application is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.
