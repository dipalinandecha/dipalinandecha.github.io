Privacy Policy

For Android apps published by Dipali Nandecha

Effective from 1 October 2026 | Last updated 1 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how your information is handled when using our mobile application. It applies to all users regardless of language preference.

Summary: You do not need an account, no personal identifiable information is sent to our private servers, and external data transmission is limited strictly to Google services for serving advertisements and basic stability reporting.

Apps Covered by this Policy

  • Hindi Panchang and Calendar (com.hindipanchang.calendar)

Information the App Collects

Information You Provide

None. The app does not ask you to register, sign in, or provide a name, email address, or phone number. Your settings—such as chosen city, reminder alarms, and festival preferences—are stored locally on your device and are removed if you uninstall the app or clear app storage.

Location Data

Panchang calculations (such as Tithi, Shubh Muhurat, Rahu Kaal, Sunrise, and Sunset timings) vary depending on geographical location. The app allows you to manually pick your city from an offline list. If you choose automatic detection, the app requests Android's location permission only to match the nearest city for calculations. Your location coordinates are processed locally and are never stored on external servers operated by us.

Advertising & Diagnostics

The app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob and uses Google Play Services. Through these libraries, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's Advertising ID and relevant hardware identifiers;
  • Your IP address (used to infer coarse/approximate geographic location);
  • Device parameters (operating system version, screen resolution, model);
  • Ad interaction and performance metrics;
  • Crash logs and diagnostic reports to resolve software issues.

This information is visible to us only as aggregated counts and statistics. We cannot access your files, messages, or device contacts.

Advertising

Advertisements support this free application. Ad serving is governed by Google AdMob policies. You can learn more by reviewing how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time via your device settings: Settings > Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads).

Device Permissions Requested

  • Internet and Network State: Required to fetch ad inventory and keep festival databases current.
  • Location (Optional): Used solely to identify your closest city for sunrise, sunset, and panchang calculation.
  • Notifications (Optional): Used to trigger localized alerts for chosen Vrats, Tithis, and festivals.

Children's Privacy

This application is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
