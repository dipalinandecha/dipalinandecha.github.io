Privacy Policy

For Android apps published by Dipali Nandecha

Effective from 1 October 2026 | Last updated 1 October 2026

This privacy policy explains how information is handled when using our Telugu Calendar and Panchangam application.

Summary: No registration is required, personal information is never uploaded to our servers, and third-party interactions are limited to Google AdMob for ad delivery and diagnostic logging.

Apps Covered by this Policy

  • Telugu Calendar and Panchangam (com.telugucalendarpanchangam)

Information the App Collects

Information You Provide

None. You can use all core features without creating an account or providing contact details. User preferences and festival reminders are maintained strictly on your device.

Location Data

Telugu Panchangam details (including Rahu Kalam, Yamagandam, Gulika Kalam, Tithi, and Nakshatram timings) depend on astronomical coordinates. The app lets you choose your city manually. If location permission is granted, coordinates are utilized strictly on-device to select the nearest astronomical reference city.

Advertising & Diagnostics

Advertisements are served through Google AdMob. Google may gather:

  • Device advertising identifiers;
  • IP address for rough location estimation;
  • Interaction statistics with ad impressions;
  • Crash and application stability diagnostics.

Review how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy for full details on their processing.

Device Permissions Requested

  • Internet & Network State: Used to serve advertisements and synchronize panchang tables.
  • Location (Optional): Used to calculate city-specific Rahu Kalam and Tithi timings accurately.
  • Notifications (Optional): Used for reminder notifications of selected festivals and Ekadashi/Purnima dates.

Children's Privacy

This application is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.
